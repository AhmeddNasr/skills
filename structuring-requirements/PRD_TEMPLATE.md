# Product Requirements Document (PRD) Template

Use this template to generate a comprehensive PRD before breaking down features into granular User Stories.

## 1. Title & Meta
*   **Project Name:** [Name]
*   **Target Release:** [Date / Version / Sprint]
*   **ADO Epic Link:** [Placeholder for ADO Link]
*   **Legacy TFS Reference (If applicable):** [Placeholder for TFS Link]

## 2. Objective & Overview
*Briefly, what is this project about? Why are we doing it? What core business problem does it solve?*

## 3. Target Personas
*Who is this for? Identify the primary and secondary users.*

## 4. Success Metrics & Goals
*How do we know it worked? Define quantitative and qualitative KPIs.*

## 5. Assumptions & Constraints
*What are we assuming to be true? What are our technical, budget, or compliance limits?*

## 6. Key User Scenarios (High-Level Workflows)
*Full stories about how various personas will use the product in context.*

## 7. Features In Scope (The Requirements)
*High-level list of distinct, prioritized features along with a short explanation as to why this feature is important.*

## 8. Features Out of Scope
*What have we explicitly decided NOT to do and why? (Crucial for preventing scope creep).*

## 9. Designs & UX
*Links to Figma mockups, wireframes, or design system components.*

## 10. Open Issues & Q&A
*What factors do we still need to figure out? Common questions about the product along with the decided answers.*
