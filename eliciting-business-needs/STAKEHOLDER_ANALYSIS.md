# Stakeholder Analysis Reference

Use these frameworks to identify, classify, and manage stakeholders before and during elicitation. Missing stakeholders is the most common silent failure in business analysis.

---

## 1. Power/Interest Grid

Classify stakeholders on two axes to determine your engagement strategy:

| | **Low Interest** | **High Interest** |
|---|---|---|
| **High Power** | **Keep Satisfied** — Meet their needs; don't bore them with details. | **Manage Closely** — Full engagement; key decision-makers. |
| **Low Power** | **Monitor** — Minimal effort; keep informed if needed. | **Keep Informed** — Update regularly; potential advocates or resistors. |

**How to apply:**
1. List every stakeholder affected by the change.
2. Plot them on the grid based on their organizational power and their interest in the specific initiative.
3. Allocate your elicitation time proportionally: "Manage Closely" gets the most face time; "Monitor" gets the least.

---

## 2. RACI Matrix

Define accountability for every deliverable or decision to prevent gaps and conflicts:

| Role | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **R — Responsible** | The person(s) doing the work to complete the task. |
| **A — Accountable** | The single person who owns the outcome and has final sign-off authority. Only **one** person can be Accountable. |
| **C — Consulted** | Subject-matter experts whose input is sought *before* the work is done (two-way communication). |
| **I — Informed** | People kept up to date on progress or decisions *after* the fact (one-way communication). |

**How to apply:**
1. List all major deliverables, decisions, or work packages down the left column.
2. List all stakeholder roles across the top row.
3. Assign exactly one **A** per row. Every row must have at least one **R**.
4. Flag any row with no **A** (accountability gap) or multiple **A**s (conflict).

---

## 3. Persona Creation

For user-facing requirements, create a lightweight persona for each distinct user type:

- **Name & Role:** e.g., "Sara, Senior Claims Processor"
- **Goals:** What they need to accomplish (not how).
- **Pain Points:** Current frustrations or workarounds.
- **Technical Proficiency:** Low / Medium / High — affects UX requirements.
- **Frequency of Use:** Daily / Weekly / Occasional — affects performance requirements.

**Rule:** Do not create more than 3-5 personas. Beyond that, consolidate similar profiles to keep requirements focused.
