# Stakeholder Interview Template

Use these structured questions to elicit genuine business needs and uncover unstated expectations. Do not accept superficial answers.

## Discovery Questions
*   Walk me through your current process for [Task]. Where do you spend the most time?
*   What is the primary trigger or input for this process?
*   What is the ultimate output, and who consumes it?

## Pain Point Questions
*   If you had a magic wand and could fix one thing about this workflow today, what would it be?
*   What manual workarounds or "shadow IT" (spreadsheets, sticky notes) are you currently using because the system doesn't do what you need?
*   When this process fails, what is the business impact (time lost, revenue lost, compliance risk)?

## Vision & Value Questions
*   How will you measure the success of this new solution? What specific metric will improve?
*   If we implement this feature perfectly, what will you be able to do that you cannot do today?

## Push-Back Questions (Use when requirements are vague)
*   You mentioned needing a "faster" system. What is the exact acceptable response time in milliseconds/seconds?
*   You asked for "AI." What specific decision or prediction do you need the system to make for you?
