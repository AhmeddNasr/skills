# Root Cause Analysis: The 5 Whys

When a stakeholder presents a problem, it is often just a symptom. Use this framework to drill down to the actual business need.

## The Process
1. State the problem as the stakeholder presented it.
2. Ask "Why did this happen?" or "Why is this a problem?"
3. Take that answer, and ask "Why?" again.
4. Repeat this at least 5 times, or until you reach a fundamental process, system, or human error that can be addressed by a solution.

## Example
*   **Stated Problem:** "The reports are always late."
*   **Why 1:** "Why are they late?" -> "Because it takes the team too long to compile the data."
*   **Why 2:** "Why does it take too long?" -> "Because the data is spread across three different legacy systems."
*   **Why 3:** "Why is it in three systems?" -> "Because the marketing, sales, and support teams use different software."
*   **Why 4:** "Why don't they use the same software?" -> "Because we never integrated them after the merger two years ago."
*   **Why 5:** "Why weren't they integrated?" -> "Because there was no defined data governance strategy."
*   **Root Cause/True Business Need:** The organization needs a unified data governance strategy and an integrated data warehouse, not just "faster reporting software."
