Use this format to synthesize your market research findings. The goal is to identify strategic advantages for the user's proposed project.

## W - Weaknesses (Competitors)

*   [Competitor B]: [Weakness]

## O - Opportunities (Our Project)
*This is the most critical section.* Based on the weaknesses identified above, what gaps exist in the market? What pain points can our proposed solution solve that no one else is currently solving well?

## T - Threats (Our Project)
*What external factors could derail this project?*
*   High switching costs for existing users.
*   Aggressive pricing from incumbents.
*   Strict regulatory compliance requirements (e.g., HIPAA, GDPR) that increase development time.
